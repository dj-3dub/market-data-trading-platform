# Reliability Engineering Focus

This project is designed not only as a trading-style microservices platform, but also as a practical environment for reliability engineering, production-style troubleshooting, and operational improvement.

## Reliability Goals
- Reduce mean time to detect issues
- Reduce mean time to recover services
- Improve observability across service boundaries
- Validate dependency awareness under failure conditions
- Document recovery workflows clearly enough to be repeatable

## Reliability Themes
- Service dependency mapping
- Health validation
- Failure scenario simulation
- Metrics-driven diagnosis
- Recovery runbooks
- Post-incident learning

## Why This Matters
In trading and execution-oriented systems, healthy infrastructure is not enough. Services must be observable, dependencies must be understood, and recovery procedures must be fast and repeatable under pressure.
